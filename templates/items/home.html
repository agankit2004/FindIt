How it works

Step one Post it

Photo, category, where and roughly when. Two minutes.

Step two We match it

Every new report is scored against the opposite board. Strong hits notify both sides.

Step three Prove and collect

Describe a detail that isn't in the listing. The finder approves, then you arrange the handover.

A note on honesty

Found something? Hold on to the one detail nobody could guess from a photo. That's what stops the wrong person walking off with it.
